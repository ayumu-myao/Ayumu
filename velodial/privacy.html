VeloDial — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

VeloDial ("the app") is a gear-ratio and lap-time calculator for track cycling, developed by MYAO LLC ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: the app does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to us. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not operate a server for VeloDial. We never receive your inputs, history, or location. No personal identifiers, no usage tracking, no advertising IDs.

2. Location data

If you tap the location button, the app reads your device's current GPS coordinates once and uses them only to look up, in real time:

  • the name of the nearest velodrome / track,
  • the ground elevation at your position, and
  • current temperature (for air-density correction).

Your coordinates are sent directly from your device to the public, key-free APIs listed below, solely to return that information. They are not stored on any server we control and never associated with your identity. Location access is entirely optional — every calculation works if you enter altitude and temperature by hand.

Third-party services queried

  • OpenStreetMap Overpass API — nearby velodrome lookup
  • Open-Elevation / OpenTopoData — ground elevation
  • Open-Meteo — temperature
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim — place name

These are independent services with their own privacy practices. We send only the coordinates needed for the lookup.

3. Data stored on your device

Your gear settings, environment values, and saved session history are stored only on your device (local app storage). They never leave your iPhone unless you choose to export a CSV and share it yourself. Deleting the app removes this data.

4. Children's privacy

VeloDial is a utility app suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: MYAO LLC — myao-llc.com/contact

位置情報について

位置情報ボタンを押した場合のみ、端末のGPS座標を一度だけ読み取り、(1)最寄りの競技場名、(2)標高、(3)気温（空気密度補正用）をその場で取得するために使います。座標は下記の公開API（キー不要）へ端末から直接送信され、当社のサーバーには保存されず、個人の特定にも使いません。位置情報の利用は任意で、標高・気温を手入力すればすべての計算が動作します。

利用API：OpenStreetMap Overpass / Open-Elevation・OpenTopoData / Open-Meteo / Nominatim

端末内のデータ

ギア設定・環境値・保存した履歴は端末内のみに保存され、利用者がCSVを書き出して共有しない限り端末外に出ません。アプリを削除すると消去されます。
