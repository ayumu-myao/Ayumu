Gear-ratio & lap-time calculator for track cycling

What VeloDial does

VeloDial turns a chainring × sprocket combination into the numbers that matter on the track: gear ratio, roll-out (development), speed at a given cadence, and predicted lap / event times — with air-density correction from altitude and temperature.

Quick start

  • Set the gear — drag the chainring and sprocket dials, or tap − / +. Front 45–70T, rear 10–20T.
  • Pick the track length — 250 / 333.33 / 400 / 500 m.
  • Choose the tire — 700×19C … 35C, or fine-tune width.
  • Set cadence — 80–145 rpm. Speed and roll-out update live.
  • Event times — 200 m / 500 m / 1 km / 3 km / 4 km, with a half-lap split table you can edit per lap.
  • Environment — enter altitude & temperature by hand, or tap the location button to auto-fill velodrome name, elevation and temperature.
  • Save & export — store a session to history and export your records as CSV.
  • Theme & language — Outdoor (light) / Dark, Japanese / English.

FAQ

Do I need to allow location?

No. Location only auto-fills velodrome name, altitude and temperature. You can type those in manually and every calculation still works.

Does it work offline?

Yes — all calculations run on-device. Only the optional location lookup needs a connection.

How is speed calculated?

Gear ratio × wheel circumference × cadence, then corrected for air density derived from altitude and temperature (standard-atmosphere model).

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

よくある質問

位置情報は必須？

いいえ。競技場名・標高・気温の自動入力に使うだけで、手入力すれば全機能動きます。

オフラインで使える？

はい。計算はすべて端末内で動作します。位置情報の取得時のみ通信が必要です。
